What Is Microblading?

You might have heard about another technique, which is called microblading in San Diego. Let us tell you about it and how it is connected with our unique service. In San Diego, Microblading (semi-permanent makeup technique) is an advanced eyebrow treatment where a thin blade is used to create thin, hair-like marks on the skin. The artist makes small cuts and drives the pigment into the epidermis. Microblading brows typically last about 18 months.

If you are planning to do Microblading in San Diego for a full and natural brow to match your skin tone, and to enhance the shape and thickness of your eyebrows, we recommend that you do the Realistic Nano Hairstrokes brow procedure. It is way less painful than microblading because a micro-needle used during the procedure ‘cuts’ the skin less than a microblade. Moreover, the Realistic Nano Hairstrokes technique lasts longer than eyebrow microblading in San Diego. They are the same beautiful, sharp hair strokes, but in a safer manner and with no color surprises.

Microblading-Replacing Brow Technique Process

The Realistic Nano Hairstrokes technique, which has a similar basis to microblading in San Diego, is carried out in several stages.

  1. Consultation. Everything starts here. There is a lot to discuss during the consultation: contraindications, the desired technique, the most suitable eyebrow style, shape, pigment color, and your expectations. You can bring some relevant pictures.
  2. Sketch and color selection. You can tell the artist what you would like to emphasize in your eyebrows, and they advise on the shape and the shade of pigment. The artist studies your face: facial expressions, structure, skin type, and then makes a sketch (now it looks like the procedure for eyebrow microblading in San Diego). To ensure your eyebrows are as symmetrical as possible and their shape is perfect, an artist uses San Diego brow mapping. The artist is outlining the eyebrows on your skin with a white pencil to resemble the look of your future eyebrows. The method shows the future brow shape to confirm that they meet expectations. Also, it is useful when a client has uneven brows.
  3. Correction. The artist removes excess hair beyond the boundaries of the pencil sketch.
  4. Disinfection and anesthesia. Your eyebrows are treated with a disinfectant solution, after which they are given local anesthesia – a numbing cream is applied for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Introduction of pigment. With small and precise strokes, the artist makes strokes with a permanent makeup machine. The micro-needle carefully injects the pigment into the right layer of the epidermis with little punctures. Gradually, the eyebrow is made denser with fine hair strokes. One, two, or even three shades of pigment can be used to create a single, double, or triple-layer eyebrow, essentially giving a 3D eyebrow effect.
  6. Completion of the procedure. In the end, the permanent makeup artist removes excess pigment, re-disinfects the skin, and applies an ointment with a healing effect.

This technique for eyebrow microblading in San Diego requires exceptional sterility of gloves, tools, and supplies. Try to pay attention to this important fact before doing the procedure.

Typically, a Realistic Nano Hairstrokes treatment that replaces microblading in San Diego takes between 3–3.5 hours, depending on the desired results. After the procedure, your eyebrows tend to look darker than the final result. So, don’t be upset if you see your eyebrows a little bit darker than you expected after the treatment.

After 10–14 days, the eyebrows show their final look and are fully healed. The typical Realistic Nano Hairstrokes treatment effect will last about 18 months. It’s recommended we meet 24 months after your treatment for a consultation to see if a touch-up is required at this point. Eventually, your eyebrows will fade out if you don’t do any of the touch-up treatments.

Pros and Cons of Services

There are a lot of clients who choose microblading in San Diego just because they are used to it. We would like to tell you more about both procedures so that you can make a choice for yourself.

Here are some advantages and disadvantages of the Microblading in San Diego treatment.

Pros

  • Comfort. It saves you time in the morning. You don’t need to draw eyebrows every day.
  • Symmetrical, well-groomed, bright eyebrows make the face look good.
  • In San Diego microblading technique can be a beauty salvation for fans of sports and outdoor activities: in the most extreme conditions, the eyebrows will not lose their color under the influence of water, sunlight, or too hot or cold air.

Cons

  • Long healing period. The machine used during this best microblading in San Diego procedure makes cuts on the skin, which means it needs more time to heal.
  • Distorted eyebrows. If the pigment penetration is not deep enough, it will “travel” over the skin, and change the direction of the drawn hairs, in a word, the client can be left with distorted brows.

They say the color changes after the cheap Microblading in San Diego and the eyebrows become blueish, reddish, and so on. After the Procedure

In San Diego, Microblading can normally result in a color change or partial color loss. As the procedure area heals, the color will lighten and might seem to disappear. This can all be corrected during the touch-up appointment, which is why the touch-up is necessary. The procedure area has to be completely healed before it. This takes about four weeks. While in San Diego, microblading requires a longer full healing time, up to 8 weeks.

After the procedure, there might be some redness and slight swelling. The swelling will lessen in 60 minutes. The regenerative process takes about 7-10 days. After this period, a light crust will appear, which peels off by itself.

Can I exercise after getting microblading?

You must avoid working out for 7 days following your microblading appointment. Light exercising is allowed (minimal sweating), but you must clean the tattooed area with lukewarm water as soon as you are done exercising. The salt in sweat will fade the pigment very quickly and give poor results.

Can I get tattooed if I am pregnant?

As a precaution to avoid any complications, we do not perform cosmetic tattooing on pregnant women.
