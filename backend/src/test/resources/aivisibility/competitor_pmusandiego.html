What problems can Lip Blush solve?
Lip Blush is the perfect solution. By implanting pigment into the lips upper layers, we can correct asymmetry, add fullness, and create a long-lasting, uniform color that stays put throughout the day.
Additionally, our pigments are carefully selected to complement your skin tone, ensuring a flawless finish that lasts for years to come.

What problems can Eyeliner Tattoo solve?
Our Eyeliner Tattoo services offer the ideal solution. By implanting pigment into the lash line, we can create a flawless, long-lasting eyeliner look that stays put from dawn till dusk.
