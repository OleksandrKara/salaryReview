# California compliance review (research notes, 2026-11-03)

Scope: Yana Kovalenko and future W-2 hourly nail technicians in San Diego. Industry wage order: IWC Order 2-2001 (personal service: nail and beauty). This is a research-based review against primary law and published guidance, not a licensed attorney's opinion. Items marked CPA/COUNSEL should be reviewed once by the owner's CPA or an employment attorney; the policy lives in one class (`CaWagePolicy`) so changes are cheap.

| # | Rule | Source | How the system handles it | Status |
|---|------|--------|---------------------------|--------|
| 1 | No work over 5 hours without a 30 min meal that starts before the end of the 5th hour; second meal over 10 hours | Labor Code 512, Wage Order 2 s.11, Brinker | `MEAL_MISSING`; a gap after the 5th hour does not cure it | Implemented in policy |
| 2 | Meal waiver only if the day is at most 6 hours AND both sides consent; employer carries the burden of proof | Labor Code 512 | Waiver stored per day with who and when (audited); owner is advised to keep a signed written waiver | CPA/COUNSEL: confirm written form |
| 3 | Meal period must be a true duty-free period; time is not rounded; records must show actual times | Donohue v. AMN, Wage Order 2 s.7 | No rounding anywhere. Pre-filled meal times are a proposal only: the technician must answer for each break (took as shown / took at another time / did not take). Never auto-assume. Records showing a violation create a presumption against the employer, so findings are shown, never hidden | Design risk, mitigated |
| 4 | 10 min PAID rest per 4 hours or major fraction (over 3.5 to 6 h: 1, over 6 to 10: 2, over 10 to 14: 3); rest is separate from the meal | Wage Order 2 s.12, Labor Code 226.7 | `REST_MISSING`; rests are never deducted from hours | Implemented |
| 5 | Premium: 1 hour at the regular rate of compensation per day for meal violations and a separate 1 hour for rest violations; the premium is wages and goes on the paycheck and wage statement for the period | Labor Code 226.7, Ferra v. Loews (regular rate), Naranjo v. Spectrum (premiums are wages) | Premium lines in the pay period summary; regular rate = hourly rate (tips excluded); recompute if a non-discretionary bonus is ever added | Implemented; CPA to confirm payroll line item |
| 6 | Daily overtime over 8 h x1.5 and over 12 h x2; weekly over 40 h x1.5; seventh consecutive day rule | Labor Code 510, 551-552 | Separate alerts `OVERTIME_DAILY`, `DOUBLE_TIME_DAILY`, `OVERTIME_WEEKLY`, `SEVENTH_DAY` | Implemented |
| 7 | Reporting time pay: reporting for a scheduled shift and getting less than half of it means pay for half the scheduled day, at least 2 and at most 4 hours; return to work a second time in a day with less than 2 hours furnished means 2 hours | Wage Order 2 s.5 | New finding `REPORTING_TIME_REVIEW` for any reported day with under 2 paid hours, any NO_SHOW or late cancellation on a day she came in, and `SECOND_REPORT_MINIMUM` for a second block under 2 hours. The 30 minute NO_SHOW credit may be below the legal minimum if she reported for a scheduled shift. Owner decides per case; recommend a written scheduling policy (technicians report only for confirmed appointments) | CPA/COUNSEL |
| 8 | Split shift: unpaid non-meal gaps set by the employer require one extra hour at minimum wage unless the day's pay already covers minimum wage x (hours + 1) | Wage Order 2 s.4(C) | Gaps longer than 60 min (configurable) are split-shift candidates; premium = shortfall between the day's actual pay (daily overtime and double time included) and minimum wage x (paid hours + 1), capped at one hour of minimum wage. San Diego minimum wage 2026 $17.75 ($18.35 in 2027). October: 10/7 (6.5 paid hours, $120.25 earned vs $133.13 required) = $12.88 potentially owed; 10/8 (9.5 paid hours, $189.63 earned vs $186.38 required) = $0.00 because overtime pay covers it. The finding is shown as disputed, pending CPA review, and the premium is still included in pay (option A). A free-time acknowledgment date per worker (providers.free_gap_ack_signed_at) is noted in the finding as evidence only and never changes an amount. Setting `timecards.split-shift.skip-premium=true` (default false) switches the premium off for all workers; turn it on only after the CPA or counsel confirms client-booked gaps are not employer-established | CPA/COUNSEL (whether client-booked gaps count as employer-established) |
| 9 | Time free of all duty is not hours worked, but she must really be free (not on call, not required to stay or answer) | Wage Order 2 s.2 (hours worked), Morillion line of cases | Owner confirmed on 2026-11-02 that technicians are free to leave. Put it in the written policy; no work messages or calls during gaps. SMS reminders are sent outside the typical gaps (08:00 and 16:00 PT) | Operating rule, owner |
| 10 | Tips belong to the employee; no deduction of card processing fees from tips | Labor Code 351, DLSE | For W-2 providers the system pays 100 percent of card tips. The commission engine's processing fee deduction on card tips MUST NOT apply to W-2 providers. Tips never count toward minimum wage | Important change vs current engine |
| 11 | Paid sick leave: 1 hour per 30 hours worked (San Diego earned sick leave; cap 80 hours, 40 usable per year) | CA Labor Code 246, San Diego Municipal Code | Out of scope for the app; hours report feeds the payroll provider which tracks accrual. Summary shows hours worked | Payroll provider/CPA |
| 12 | Wage statements, semimonthly paydays, final pay, hiring notice (Labor Code 2810.5), expense reimbursement for required tools (Labor Code 2802), records kept at least 3 years | Labor Code 226, 204, 2810.5, 2802, 1174 | Outside the app except retention (4 years) and the premium lines; owner checklist for onboarding | CPA |
| 13 | Employee must not be discouraged from taking breaks | Labor Code 226.7 | Reminder text never nags about speed; managers see missing breaks as a scheduling problem (`MEAL_NOT_POSSIBLE`) | Operating rule |

Open legal judgement calls for the owner's CPA or counsel (shortlist): items 2, 7, 8, 5 (payroll line item), 12.

Rest breaks recorded. These are decisions for the owner, not automatic.
